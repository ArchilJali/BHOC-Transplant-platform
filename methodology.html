Evidence method

Keep the source and the question apart.

A study of perfusion or another oxygen carrier can inform a question for BHOC. It cannot establish a result for BHOC. This distinction is shown at record level.

Record discipline

What we record.

  • Original DOI or PubMed URL, publication year and authors.
  • Organ, modality, study design and study population.
  • The finding attributed to its source, plus uncertainty and limits.
  • Which question it raises for BHOC, clearly marked as interpretation.
  • A status update when the source record is corrected or replaced.
