# BHOC Transplant | Evidence platform

Public transplant evidence and research context for [BHOC Transplant](https://bhoctransplant.com/).

## What lives here

- **Related evidence:** external studies on transplantation, preservation, perfusion and reperfusion. These studies provide context and testable research questions. They are not BHOC efficacy or safety evidence.
- **Current updates:** research summaries specific to the transplant pathway, with a clear publication date, model, outcome and limitations.
- **Methodology:** source identifiers, evidence type, status and the boundary between published findings and BHOC research concepts.

The first collection contains five external studies migrated from the existing `BHOC-platform/transplant/Transplant-relevant-evidence.json` record on 26 September 2026. Its DOI links and original evidence boundaries are preserved in [data/related-evidence.json](data/related-evidence.json).

## What stays in the main platform

The historical catalogue of **53 transplantation and oxygen-carrier publications** remains at the [BHOC Platform Transplant history and source catalogue](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-platform/transplant/Transplant-index.html). General HBOC history and the main human and veterinary bibliographies also remain there. This repository links to those records and does not keep a second editable copy.
